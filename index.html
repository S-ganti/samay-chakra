Controls

World

Crowd energy changes how each dance floor moves: at 0 people talk and sway, at 1 most dance, towards 2 they jump and raise their hands. Dancers step on the beat of the music. Render style redraws the world (Medium and High): Fusion Series draws each chapter in its poster's folk × modern style; Sumi & Shu is ink wash on rice paper where only vermilion survives; Chungking Neon lets one colour own the frame, with step-printed smear, halation and scope bars; Painted Light is brushwork with coloured shade and light as the lead. Realism blends each chapter's poster palette with a physically based sky and sun (1 is the designed mix, 2 is fully physical by day). Light shafts, contact shadows, depth of field and grass run on Medium and High quality; High also gives the trees fuller crowns and shadows. Chapter blend length sets how much of each 3-hour prahar is spent crossing into the next one; light, fog, crowd and music all follow it. Weather follows Bengaluru's climate for today's date: afternoon storms before the monsoon, showers from June to October, misty winter dawns. The moon shows tonight's real phase. On warm nights fireflies come out, and over several minutes they fall into step and flash together.

Music

Your tracks replace the engine for their chapter and crossfade with the neighbours. Load a file or drop it on a chapter row (or anywhere on the scene for the chapter on screen). Files play from your device and are never uploaded. Sounds of the hour is a synthesised soundscape under the music that follows the clock and the weather: crickets and owls, temple bells and a conch before dawn, koels, crows, the city, fire, rain and thunder. Set the raga engine to 0 to hear only the place.

Capture

Recording captures the frame above plus the music. It saves as MP4 where your browser supports it (Chrome, Safari), otherwise as WebM. Press H to hide the interface while you record. Poster postcard sets this moment in the posters' layout (3:4, with the chapter, raga, date, weather and moon) and saves it as a PNG.
