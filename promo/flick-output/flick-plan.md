## Format
- **9:16** at 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 62 s (Reels, Shorts, TikTok).
- **16:9** at 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 92 s (YouTube, LinkedIn, X).

Each scene is built once with a layout that responds to the format, and registered as two compositions (`…-v` and `…-h`).

## Brand assets
All of these come from the project itself:
- `brand-assets/ring-logo.svg`: the site favicon (the ring with eight spokes).
- The site's typefaces (Google Fonts, OFL): Cinzel (display), Jost (sans), Big Shoulders Stencil (stencil chapter), Doto (dot-matrix clock), JetBrains Mono, and the Noto scripts for each prahar's native-language title.
- Site palette: ink `#0f0d0b`, bone `#ede3d1`, ember `#ff7a2f`, sindoor `#d8342b`, plus each chapter's key colour (Enter `#ff8a3d`, Gathering `#e0332a`, Eclipse `#ff3d9a`, Brahma `#7fa2e6`, Diamond `#ffc247`, Dispersal `#d8342b`, Zero `#e9b43b`, Return `#f0a060`).
- Website footage captured from the real site (`../footage/`): eight prahar clips, a 24 h time-lapse, five style renders of the same shot, carving, rain, moon, crowd, and the site's real interface (HUD, title card, controls panel, dial scrub).

## Creative direction
**Your direction:** "clean fluid artistic motion graphics in the style of the whole website", using snippets from the actual website.

**Flick's interpretation:**
- The real footage carries every scene. Motion graphics stay thin and typographic, the way the site's own UI does it: hairline rules, wide-tracked uppercase Jost, Cinzel titles, native script in the chapter colour, and the ring motif.
- Motion is eased and fluid: masks, line-draws and slow push-ins. Nothing bouncy and no generic "AI" gradients.
- One recurring device, the **ring progress dial**, ties it together: it fills one-eighth per prahar.
- **Exception to Flick's rule:** Flick normally adds no background music. You chose the site's own raga engine as the soundtrack. It is laid under the assembled cut, not inside the scenes, and it changes raga at every prahar. Flick's bundled SFX are used only on the few hard visual hits listed below, mixed low.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — hook-not-a-game — 0.0–1.4 (h 0.0–1.6)
**Transcript:** "This isn't a video game."

**What's on screen:** Full-bleed dusk footage of the portal and procession (Enter the Ring), already in motion on frame 1 with a slow push-in. The line sits centre-low in Cinzel caps, with a thin bone hairline underneath.

**Text on screen:** THIS ISN'T A VIDEO GAME.

**Brand assets / supplied material:** `s02_enter`.

**Sequential / interaction:** The text is fully visible on frame 1; no fade-in, so it reads at 0 s. On "GAME" a sindoor strike-through line draws across the word at 1.0 s.

**Sound effect:** none (the raga starts at 0).

**Audio-coupled idea:** The strike-through lands on a beat.

**Transition:** A hard cut on the beat.

### Scene 2 — hook-its-a-clock — 1.4–3.2 (h 1.6–3.6)
**Transcript:** "It's a clock."

**What's on screen:** The 24 h time-lapse of the ring: sky cycling dusk → night → dawn → noon. Overlaid, the site's ring dial (the favicon geometry) draws itself, and a hand sweeps a full circle in sync with the time-lapse. A Doto dot-matrix clock reads 18:00 → 17:59.

**Text on screen:** IT'S A CLOCK. plus the running clock digits.

**Brand assets / supplied material:** `s01_timelapse_day`, `ring-logo.svg` geometry, the Doto font.

**Sequential / interaction:** The dial line-draws in 0.3 s, the hand sweeps 360°, and the digits count.

**Sound effect:** `Impact.mp3`, quiet, at the cut-in.

**Audio-coupled idea:** The impact lands as the dial completes its first draw.

**Transition:** The dial stays on screen and scales into the next scene, where it becomes the 8-segment ring.

### Scene 3 — eight-prahars-ring — 3.2–6.5 (h 3.6–7.6)
**Transcript:** "Ancient India split the day into 8 prahars."

**What's on screen:** On ink black with faint film grain, the ring splits into 8 arcs. Each arc lights in its chapter colour in clockwise order, and as it lights, that prahar's native-script name fades in beside it: प्रवेश, محفل, གཟའ་འཛིན, ബ്രഹ്മമുഹൂർത്തം, প্রভাত, ವಿಸರ್ಜನೆ, ଛାୟାହୀନ, திரும்புதல். The line sits above the ring.

**Text on screen:** ANCIENT INDIA SPLIT THE DAY INTO **8 PRAHARS**. The "8" is in ember.

**Brand assets / supplied material:** The ring geometry, the Noto script fonts, the chapter colours.

**Sequential / interaction:** The 8 arcs light one by one, 0.2 s apart. The full ring holds for the final 1 s.

**Sound effect:** `Pop.mp3`, very quiet, on the first and last arc only.

**Audio-coupled idea:** The arcs light on eighth-notes of the raga.

**Transition:** A zoom through the ring's centre into the footage.

### Scene 4 — raga-light-crowd — 6.5–9.5 (h 7.6–11.6)
**Transcript:** "Each with its own raga. Its own light. Its own crowd."

**What's on screen:** Three fast full-bleed cuts, 1 s each, each with one phrase:
- Gathering crimson, with ITS OWN RAGA and a small animated waveform hairline.
- Brahma Muhurta indigo with star trails, with ITS OWN LIGHT.
- The crowd dancing, with ITS OWN CROWD.

**Text on screen:** EACH WITH ITS OWN RAGA. / ITS OWN LIGHT. / ITS OWN CROWD.

**Brand assets / supplied material:** `s03_gathering`, `s05_brahma`, `s10_crowd_dance`.

**Sequential / interaction:** Each phrase wipes in left to right behind a hairline mask.

**Sound effect:** none.

**Audio-coupled idea:** The cuts land on beats.

**Transition:** Hard cuts.

### Scene 5 — i-built-all-eight — 9.5–10.5 (h 11.6–13.0)
**Transcript:** "I built all eight."

**What's on screen:** The ring motif shrinks to a small progress dial in the top corner, showing 0/8. The centre of the frame holds the line over a darkened, blurred frame of the first prahar.

**Text on screen:** I BUILT ALL EIGHT.

**Brand assets / supplied material:** `s02_enter` (blurred).

**Sequential / interaction:** The dial travels to its corner position and stays there for the whole montage.

**Sound effect:** `Click.mp3`, quiet, when the dial docks.

**Audio-coupled idea:** The dock click lands on the downbeat of the chapter music.

**Transition:** The blur pulls to sharp and the montage begins.

### Scenes 6–13 — prahar-enter, prahar-gathering, prahar-eclipse, prahar-brahma, prahar-diamond, prahar-dispersal, prahar-zero, prahar-return — 3.5 s each (h 5.5 s each)
**Transcript:** "[Title] · [hours] · Raga [name]"

**What's on screen:** That prahar's footage, full-bleed, with a slow drift. The site's own chapter-card typography is recreated over it:
- The small eyebrow "THE RING / SAMAY CHAKRA".
- The big title in the chapter's own face: Cinzel for Gathering, Eclipse and Diamond; Jost Light, wide-tracked, for Enter, Brahma, Zero and Return; Big Shoulders Stencil for Dispersal.
- The native-script name in the chapter colour.
- The hours and raga in wide-tracked caps.

The corner dial fills one more eighth in the chapter colour.

**Text on screen:** For example, ENTER THE RING / प्रवेश / 18:00 – 21:00 · RAGA YAMAN.

**Brand assets / supplied material:** `s02`–`s09` footage, the site fonts, the chapter colours.

**Sequential / interaction:** Title letters unmask upward and stagger. The native script fades in at +0.25 s, the meta line at +0.45 s, and the dial segment fills over 0.6 s. The text holds for at least 1.6 s (16:9: 3 s) and fades before the cut.

**Sound effect:** none. The music itself changes raga at each cut.

**Audio-coupled idea:** Each cut lands on the first downbeat of the next prahar's raga.

**Transition:** A circular iris wipe in the next chapter's colour, centred on the corner dial (a thin coloured ring sweeps the frame open).

**Transcript:** "No app. No download. It runs in your browser."

### Scene 18 — which-prahar-are-you — 57.0–62.0 (h 85.0–92.0)
**Transcript:** "Samay Chakra · The Ring. Which prahar are you?"

**What's on screen:**
1. On ink black, the ring logo line-draws with its 8 spokes, and the 8 chapter colours flicker once around it.
2. SAMAY CHAKRA appears in Cinzel, with THE RING in tracked Jost, and the URL below.
3. In the last 1.5 s, the question WHICH PRAHAR ARE YOU? replaces the URL, and the frame cross-dissolves toward the dusk portal (scene 1's first frame) so the loop is seamless.

**Text on screen:** SAMAY CHAKRA / THE RING / s-ganti.github.io/samay-chakra / WHICH PRAHAR ARE YOU? (comment below)

**Brand assets / supplied material:** `ring-logo.svg`, `s02_enter` (first frame, for the loop).

**Sequential / interaction:** The logo draws, then the wordmark appears, then the URL, then the question replaces it.

**Sound effect:** `Impact.mp3`, quiet, when the logo completes.

**Audio-coupled idea:** The logo completes on the final downbeat. The raga then fades, but not to silence, so the loop restart is not abrupt.

**Transition:** A loop dissolve into scene 1.

## Assembly (outside Flick, because Flick renders scenes separately)
The rendered scenes are concatenated with ffmpeg in transcript order for each format. The audio is built from the per-prahar raga recordings:
- A continuous bed under scenes 1–5.
- The raga switches at each prahar cut in scenes 6–13.
- A bed under scenes 14–18.
- Short crossfades, then loudness-normalised to −14 LUFS.

## First-10-seconds check (scoring skipped, so this is a self-review)
After rendering, I inspect frames at 0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 6.0 and 9.5 s against these checks:
- The hook is readable on frame 1.
- The payoff is visible by 3 s.
- Every phrase holds for at least 0.3 s per word.
- Nothing important sits in the platform UI danger zones: the bottom ~20% and the right ~12% on 9:16.
